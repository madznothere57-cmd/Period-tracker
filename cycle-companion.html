About Fenn

Fenn lives at the edge of the thicket — a small, soft-antlered deer-spirit who's more moth than wolf: gentle, a little shy, easily delighted by small things. He's named for the fen, the marshy meadow where he was first spotted, half-asleep in the reeds.

He doesn't try to fix your mood, he just sits with it. He notices what you log — irritable days get a nudge toward the Play tab, low-energy days get permission to rest. He collects acorns, forgets where he puts them, and thinks every star in Star Smash is personally his fault for scattering.
